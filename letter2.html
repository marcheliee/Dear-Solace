Dear Solace

LETTERS FOR THE SOUL
A LITTLE REMINDER

Dear reader,

I hope you know that you are someone worth appreciating, even in ways you might not always notice. I know you probably don't think much about it, but there are so many little things about you that make you special. The way you express yourself, the way you care about the things you love, the way you get excited over something seemingly insignificant, and even the little habits you don't realize you have. These things make you who you are, and I hope you never feel the need to change them just to be accepted.

And please, don't be too hard on yourself. You don't have to be good at everything, have everything figured out, or meet everyone's expectations. You are allowed to make mistakes. You are allowed to change your mind, discover new interests, and outgrow things you once thought would stay with you forever. You don't owe anyone a perfect version of yourself.

I also hope you remember that you don't have to earn your place in someone's life by constantly giving, helping, or making yourself useful. You deserve kindness without having to ask for it, appreciation without having to prove yourself, and respect without having to beg for it. Never convince yourself that you are asking for too much simply because you want to be treated with the same consideration you give to others.

Oh, and one more thing. I hope you learn to appreciate yourself the way you appreciate the people around you. Give yourself credit for the things you do, no matter how ordinary they may seem. Be proud of your achievements, even the little ones. Celebrate the things that make you happy, wear the things you like, enjoy your favorite songs, and never feel embarrassed about being yourself.

You are more than what you can offer to other people. You are more than your achievements, your appearance, or the opinions others have of you. You are your own person, with your own thoughts, feelings, dreams, and little peculiarities. And I think that's something worth celebrating.

So, here's your little reminder: You matter, even when nobody says it out loud. You are appreciated, even in ways you may not immediately recognize. And above all, you deserve to be yourself without feeling like you have to apologize for it.

Don't forget that, okay?

With love,
Marchel
