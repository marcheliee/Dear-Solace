A LETTER WRITTEN FOR YOU

Dear Chloe,

I love you so much, and you already know that. But I don't think I'll ever get tired of reminding you.

Can you believe that we've known each other since Grade 7? And now, here we are, already in Grade 10. It feels a little surreal how quickly time has passed. It seems like just yesterday we were still figuring things out, getting used to our new environment, and slowly building the friendship we have today.

Looking back, I'm genuinely grateful that I met you. Out of all the people I could've crossed paths with, I'm glad it was you. Thank you for being one of the people who made my school years a little less ordinary. For all the laughs, the random conversations, the silly moments, the stories, and even the memories that might seem insignificant to other people but will always mean something to me.

I hope you know how much I appreciate you. Not just because you're my best friend, but because you're Chloe. You have your own way of making things better, sometimes without even realizing it. And even on days when things aren't particularly exciting, having you around makes them more memorable.

We've probably changed a lot since Grade 7. We've grown, learned new things, experienced different situations, and met different versions of ourselves along the way. But one thing I hope never changes is the friendship we've built. I hope we continue to grow alongside each other, even when life eventually takes us in different directions.

And if there are days when you doubt yourself, I hope you remember that you're appreciated more than you know. You deserve good things, genuine happiness, and people who will always choose to stay in your life. Please don't be too hard on yourself, and never think that you have to face everything alone. You have a friend in me, always.

I don't know what the coming years have in store for us. Maybe we'll end up in different classrooms, different schools, or even different places someday. Maybe we'll become busier and won't have as much time to talk as we do now. But I hope that, no matter how much things change, we'll still find our way back to each other.

I want us to have more memories to look back on. More laughter, more stupid jokes, more conversations, more pictures, and more stories that we'll probably laugh about years from now. I hope that when we're older, we'll still remember how our friendship started and how lucky we were to have each other during these years.

Thank you for staying, Chloe. Thank you for being my best friend since Grade 7 and for making these years something worth remembering.

I love you so much. More than this letter could ever explain.

Here's to more years of friendship, more memories, and a lifetime of being each other's favorite headache.

With so much love,
Your best friend.
